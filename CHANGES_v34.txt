v34 - vehicles online, PvP, new zombie respawn
==============================================
VEHICLES (vehicle.gd)
- The SERVER runs the car physics. Clients show a frozen copy that follows the
  server's snapshots (20/s), so every player sees the same car in the same place.
- The driver's phone only sends steering/throttle to the server.
- 4 seats: driver, front passenger, 2 standing in the cargo bed. Enter/Exit button
  puts you in the first free seat. Players in the bed can shoot.
- Server checks range and free seat before letting someone in. Seats are freed if
  a rider disconnects. Logs on the bed are shared by everybody.
- Zombies are run over by the server's car.

PVP (network.gd, combat.gd)
- Your bullets/knife can hit other players. Server validates range + fire rate.
- Damage = weapon damage x 0.5 (PVP_DAMAGE_MULT in network.gd).
- 3 seconds spawn protection after respawn. Red flash when you are shot.
- Kill feed ("A killed B" / "B died") on everybody's screen.
- Turn it off on the server with:  ./LastForestServer --headless --server --no-pvp

ZOMBIES (spawner.gd)
- Home zombies are spread over the whole map. A killed zombie returns after 60-120 s,
  at its own home, and only if no player is within 80 m or looking at that spot.
- Ambient wanderers: if you have fewer than 4 zombies within 100 m, one slowly
  appears from behind you / out of sight every ~15 s. They are removed when left far
  behind and never respawn.
- Population cap grows with player count (44 + 8 per extra player, max 90).
- All numbers are constants at the top of spawner.gd.

FIXES
- HUD now always shows YOUR health/food/water online (it could show another player's).
- Landscape orientation is forced on Android.

CITY / MAP (city.gd) + VEHICLE LOOK
- Players sitting in the cab (driver / front seat) are hidden; riders in the bed stay visible.
- Every ordinary city building now has a furnished SECOND FLOOR (5 variants: bedrooms,
  office, archive, survivor camp, apartment), ceiling lamps, baseboards, dim lights,
  railings around the stairwell, and 1-2 loot items upstairs.
- Old restaurant rebuilt as "Mel's Diner": checker floor, front windows and doorway,
  counter + stools, booths, kitchen, jukebox, collapsed front-right corner, neon sign.
- GAS STATION: the shop booth was a solid block (weapons were stuck inside the wall and
  unreachable). It is hollow now with a door, counter, shelves and a weapon rack.
  Loot: 3 pistols, 2 shotguns, 2 AKs and ammo for all three (+ some outside by the pumps).
