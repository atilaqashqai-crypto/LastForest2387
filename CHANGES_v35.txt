v35 - online fixes
==================
- Online: your own name is no longer shown above your own head (other players still see it).
- Online: zombies were invisible. Cause: the terrain collision is only loaded around players
  (about 70 m), but zombies stayed awake up to 90 m away. Zombies outside the loaded ground fell
  through the world before any player reached them. Now a zombie waits (frozen) until the ground
  under it is loaded, and a zombie that somehow ends up below the ground is put back on top.
  (terrain.gd: has_ground(), zombie.gd: _ground_ok())
- Zip name now has the version number: LastForest_v35.zip (+1 each update).
