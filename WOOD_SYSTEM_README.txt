PROJECT: LAST FOREST - PICKUP & WOOD HARVESTING UPDATE
Base: GptUpdate_v9.zip

PICKUP
- The previous off-road vehicle body has been redesigned as a pickup truck.
- The rear cargo bed has a floor, two side rails and a tailgate.
- The cargo bed can visually hold up to five cut logs.

WOOD HARVESTING
- Walk close to a forest tree and press the existing interact key/button four times.
- A small CUTTING counter appears above the tree.
- The tree is removed and three physics-based logs spawn around its stump.
- Walk close to a log and interact to carry one log. A small log is shown on the player.
- Walk to the rear of the pickup and interact to place the carried log in the cargo bed.
- Loaded logs remain visible in the pickup cargo bed. Capacity: five logs.

CONTROLS
- Uses the existing E key / Enter-Exit interaction button. No new movement controls are added.
- Existing player movement and camera scripts are otherwise left unchanged.

TEST STATUS
- ZIP structure and text resources checked.
- Godot runtime/editor is not installed in this execution environment, so the project has not been launched or physics-tested here.
