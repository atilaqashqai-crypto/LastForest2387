v33 - stairs, wall damage, performance (based on Gptv32 Hospital_2_Gray_Per_Floor)

STAIRS (city.gd)
- Old stairs were tall vertical boxes (CharacterBody3D cannot climb them) and two overlapping flights made a tent shape.
- Every city building now has 3 straight ramps (about 30 degrees) in the rear shaft, one per storey.
- Floors 1..3 have a matching opening where the ramp arrives. Ground floor is solid (no hole in it any more).
- Removed the stray floating "landing" box that blocked the shaft.
- Hospital: 2 ramps (ground->1st->2nd) in the corridor with matching floor openings. (Hospital still has no front door, as before.)

DAMAGE THROUGH WALLS
- zombie.gd: bite needs same floor level (max 1.6 m height difference) and two clear rays (chest + head) to the player; areas no longer interfere.
- combat.gd: knife/gun hits now need a clear line to the zombie (walls, floors, doors block; other zombies do not).

PERFORMANCE
- city.gd: all static boxes merged into a few meshes (about 1 per building instead of hundreds), shared materials, meshes hidden beyond 150 m,
  street/hospital lights only switched on within 42 m of a player.
- zombie.gd: each zombie body merged into about 8 meshes (was about 40), shared eye material, animation skipped beyond 50 m,
  height scaling no longer touches every part each frame.
- spawner.gd: activity check runs every 0.5 s (was every frame); hospital zombies sleep when the player is farther than 70 m.
- player.gd: floor_snap_length 0.5 so walking down ramps stays smooth.
Not tested in Godot (not installed here). Please test and send me the error text if anything shows up in the Output panel.

- v33d: fixed type-inference parse error in zombie.gd (_put).
- v33e: door handles now sit on the door (free edge, ~1 m high, both faces). Hospital: walls reach the next slab (no slit), fluorescent lights
